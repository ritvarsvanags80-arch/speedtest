About this speed test

This free internet speed test measures ping, jitter, download speed and upload speed directly in your browser, with no app to install. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

Live monitor is made for aiming an antenna (4G, 5G, LTE or Wi-Fi): it measures continuously and shows the 1-second speed, the 3-second average, the session average and the best position with its time. An optional tone rises in pitch as you get closer to the best speed, so you can turn the antenna without looking at the screen.

Results can be shown in Mbps or MB/s and are saved in your browser's history.

Measured against Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com). Results depend on your browser and device.
